How to Join

Carrot Creek is hosted on Minehut. Please follow all rules and guidlines while playing in our town

Carrot Creek is hosted by Gamer Dorks. To join, follow these steps:

  1. Make sure you have Minecraft installed.
  2. Copy the server link:
  3. Click add server and paste the IP or join directly using Direct connection. Both require the IP.
  4. Hop in and start building, exploring, and having fun in Carrot Creek!

Rules for Our Town

These rules apply to **Carrot Creek** specifically, so everyone in our town can have a fun and safe experience:

  • Be respectful to all players in Carrot Creek.
  • No griefing, stealing, or breaking others’ builds.
  • Participate in events and community activities whenever possible.
  • Have fun and be creative while following the spirit of our town!
